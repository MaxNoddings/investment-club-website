The Fund

Our portfolio is built around a disciplined target asset allocation and a clear set of investment guidelines.

Equities40–70%
Fixed Income10–30%
Alternative Investments0–30%
Cash & Cash Equivalents>20%

Pricing

  • No Penny Stocks
  • No Margin Trading

Market Guidelines

  • Target Large and Mid-cap companies — those with a market capitalization over $1–2 billion.
  • Keep an ESG focus in line with our sustainability commitments, limiting equities that harm the environment.
  • Limit any single equity holding to a maximum of 10%.

Investment Advisory Committee

  • Assists and guides the Student Investment Fund.
  • Holds the final veto and oversees major decisions.

Benefit to the College

A thriving student investment fund strengthens both the institution and the students who run it.

For Saint Michael's

  • A study by NACUBO found that the average endowment for colleges with investment clubs was $1.2 billion, compared to $800 million for colleges without them.
  • A study by the Council for Advancement and Support of Education (CASE) found that colleges with strong alumni engagement programs averaged $1.5 billion endowments, compared to $900 million for those with weak engagement.
  • More engagement, enthusiasm, and willingness to contribute from Saint Michael's alumni.

For Students

  • More successful graduates: a Wharton School study found that 20% of students who participated in investment clubs were admitted to a top business school, compared to only 10% of those who did not.
  • A platform to interact with peers, alumni, and industry professionals — expanding students' networks and connections.
  • A place to cultivate essential technical skills for finance careers, from market fundamentals to risk assessment and equity research.

Focus on Sustainability

Our sustainability agenda is strategically aligned with Saint Michael's dedication to environmental stewardship. We apply ESG exclusionary screens across the portfolio.

Environmental

  • Fossil fuel extraction: companies deriving 5% or more revenue from thermal coal mining or unconventional oil and gas extraction.
  • Thermal coal power: companies deriving 5% or more revenue from thermal coal-based power generation.
  • Palm oil: companies deriving 5% or more revenue from the production of palm oil.

Social

  • Tobacco: producers, or companies deriving 5% or more revenue from the production, distribution, retail, supply, and licensing of tobacco-related products.
  • Civilian firearms: producers of firearms and small-arms ammunition for the civilian market, or companies deriving 5% or more revenue from their distribution.

Governance

  • UN Global Compact: companies that are not in compliance with the United Nations Global Compact principles.
